Client Transformation — Tushar, Real Estate & Business Owner
He built companies on discipline. He’d just never turned it on himself.
Tushar built and runs companies most men only dream of owning. Malls. Real estate. Decades of discipline, applied to everything except his own body.
That changed once he decided to apply the same standard to himself that he applies to his business — consistency over intensity, the process over the excuse. The weight came off because the habits held, week after week, not because of one dramatic push.
What changed wasn’t just his body. People who’ve known him for years now read him differently — more composed, more deliberate, carrying himself like a man who’s spent years in structure and discipline. That’s not an accident. That’s what happens when the same rigor he runs his companies with finally gets pointed at himself.
